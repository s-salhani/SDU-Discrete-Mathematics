# Discrete Mathematics

Personal notes, exercises, and study material from my **Discrete Mathematics** course as part of my MSc in **Data Science – Artificial Intelligence** at the University of Southern Denmark (SDU).

This repository documents my work throughout the course and serves as a reference for concepts that form part of the mathematical foundation of computer science and artificial intelligence.

## Topics

The repository covers topics such as:

- Propositional and predicate logic
- Sets and set operations
- Functions and sequences
- Algorithms
- Number theory
- Mathematical induction
- Recursion
- Counting and combinatorics
- Relations
- Graphs and trees
- Boolean algebra

The repository is updated as I progress through the course.

## Repository Structure

The notes are written primarily in Markdown and include:

- Definitions and key concepts
- Mathematical notation
- Worked examples
- Exercise solutions
- Explanations and personal study notes

## Purpose

The purpose of this repository is both to support my studies and to document my understanding of discrete mathematics.

In particular, the material reflects concepts relevant to areas such as algorithms, data structures, logic, probability, and artificial intelligence.
